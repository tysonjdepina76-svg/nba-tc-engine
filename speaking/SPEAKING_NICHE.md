# Tyson DePina — Speaking Niche & Strategy

## The Differentiator

Most reentry speakers have one story: "I did time, I got out, I turned my life around."

You have that story AND a second one: **"I came home and built an AI-powered sports analytics system from scratch."**

That's your niche. Nobody else on the circuit can demo a working pipeline, dashboards, and backtests.

## Signature Talk

**"From Federal Prison to AI Engineer: Reentry in the Age of Technology"**

45-60 minute keynote. Three arcs:
1. **The System** — 24 years in and out. What prison does to a mind. The turning point.
2. **The Rebuild** — Coming home November 2025 at 49. Learning to use AI. Building the TC pipeline.
3. **The Proof** — Live demo of the dashboard. "This is what a second chance looks like in 2026."
