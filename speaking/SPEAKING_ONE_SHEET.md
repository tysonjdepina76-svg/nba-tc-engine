# Tyson DePina — Speaking One-Sheet

**From Federal Prison to AI Engineer: Reentry in the Age of Technology**

---

## Bio

Tyson DePina is a formerly incarcerated Cape Verdean American from New Bedford, Massachusetts. After serving 8 years in federal prison — part of 24 total years cycling through the system since 1994 — he was released in November 2025. Instead of waiting for permission, he taught himself to work with artificial intelligence and built a live sports analytics pipeline with dashboards, APIs, backtests, and automated reporting.

He is a father of three, co-founder of DNM Landscaping and Snow Removal, and author of *The Mirror Workbook* — a trauma-informed treatment workbook for system-involved youth. His tagline: "The system gave me a record. I gave myself a purpose."

---

## Signature Talks

### 1. AI & Second Chances: Reentry in the Age of Technology *(Flagship)*
How a 49-year-old with no tech background came home from federal prison and built a working AI-powered data pipeline in 8 months. For tech companies, university CS/social impact programs, and anyone who needs proof that second chances produce first-rate results.

### 2. From the System to the Solution
24 years in and out. What the carceral system does to a person — and the moment of decision that changes everything. For reentry organizations, criminal justice programs, and policy audiences.

### 3. The Mirror: Reaching Youth Before They Fall
Trauma, identity, and the workbook approach that gives system-involved kids language for pain they don't have words for yet. For juvenile justice, schools, and youth programs.

### 4. Cape Verdean Resilience
Cultural identity, community violence, and breaking generational cycles. For community organizations, cultural events, and diaspora audiences.
